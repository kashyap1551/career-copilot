Teacher of Mathematics
Hollowfield Secondary School, Tarnwick

Hollowfield Secondary School is a welcoming school of 900 students. We are looking for a teacher of mathematics to join a supportive department from September.

Required:
- Qualified teacher status and experience teaching mathematics to students aged 11 to 16.
- Able to plan lessons and schemes of work that match the national curriculum.
- Experience adapting lessons for students with special educational needs.
- Confident in assessing student progress and reporting it to parents.
- Experience leading or supporting extra-curricular activities.

A plus:
- Experience teaching A-level or equivalent further mathematics.
- Familiarity with online learning platforms.

About you:
- You are patient, enthusiastic and a positive role model for young people.

We offer regular training, a reduced timetable in your first year and a friendly staff room.
