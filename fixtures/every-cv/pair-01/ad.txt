Staff Nurse, Medical Ward
Brindlemoor Community Clinic, Tarnwick

Brindlemoor Community Clinic is a small, friendly clinic with a 24-bed medical ward. We are looking for a staff nurse to join our ward team from next month.

Required:
- Registered nurse with at least three years of experience on an adult medical or surgical ward.
- Confident in wound care and dressing changes, including complex wounds.
- Experience with medication administration and double-checking procedures.
- Able to carry out patient assessments and record vital signs accurately.
- Experience mentoring student nurses or new colleagues.

A plus:
- Familiarity with an electronic patient record system.
- Basic knowledge of infection prevention standards.

About you:
- You are a calm, reliable team player who stays patient under pressure.

We offer a permanent contract, paid training days and a supportive team. Send your application to recruiter@example.org.
