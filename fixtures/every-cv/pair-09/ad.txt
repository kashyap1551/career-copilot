Warehouse Team Member
Tarnwick Distribution Centre

Our distribution centre needs reliable people for the day shift. No two days are the same, and training is given.

Required:
- Experience picking and packing orders in a warehouse.
- Ability to operate a forklift or a pallet truck, with a valid licence.
- Experience using a handheld scanner or a warehouse management system.
- Able to lift and move goods safely.
- Basic numeracy and accurate record keeping.

A plus:
- Experience with stock counts and inventory checks.

About you:
- You are punctual, reliable and a good team player.

We offer a permanent contract, paid breaks and a safety-first culture.
