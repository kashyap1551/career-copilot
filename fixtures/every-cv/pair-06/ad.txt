Customer Service Coordinator, Logistics Office
Tarnwick Freight Partners, Musterstadt

Tarnwick Freight Partners moves goods by road and sea for customers across Europe. Our logistics office in Musterstadt is looking for a customer service coordinator.

Required:
- Two or more years of experience in customer service or order handling in logistics.
- Good knowledge of shipping documents such as bills of lading and customs forms.
- Able to track shipments and keep customers updated proactively.
- Experience with an ERP or transport management system.
- Fluent English and German, written and spoken.

A plus:
- Experience negotiating rates with carriers.
- Knowledge of customs procedures for non-EU shipments.

About you:
- You are friendly, resilient and enjoy working in a busy office.

We offer a permanent contract, a modern office and a team that supports each other.
