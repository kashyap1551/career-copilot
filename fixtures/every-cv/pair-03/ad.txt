Mechanical Design Engineer
Tarnwick Precision Works

Tarnwick Precision Works builds compact gearboxes for packaging machines. Our design team is looking for a mechanical design engineer to take parts from first sketch to production drawing.

Required:
- Degree in mechanical engineering and at least four years of design experience.
- Proficient in 3D CAD for parts and assemblies.
- Experience applying geometric dimensioning and tolerancing on production drawings.
- Experience with finite element analysis for stress and fatigue checks.
- Able to prepare cost and bill-of-materials comparisons in Excel.

A plus:
- Experience with injection moulding or sheet-metal design.
- Working knowledge of ISO 9001 quality procedures.

About you:
- You are curious, hands-on and enjoy solving problems together.

We offer a stable team, a well-equipped prototype workshop and funded training.
