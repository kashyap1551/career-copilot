Legal Assistant
Marchmont and Dunmore Solicitors, Tarnwick

We are a growing commercial law practice advising small and medium-sized companies on contracts, disputes and company matters. We are looking for a legal assistant to support our solicitors.

Required:
- At least two years of experience supporting solicitors in a commercial law practice.
- Able to draft routine contracts and letters from templates with careful attention to detail.
- Experience with legal research in statutes and case-law databases.
- Experience managing client files, deadlines and court filing dates.
- Fluent written English and the ability to summarise long documents accurately.

A plus:
- Knowledge of data protection law.
- Experience with document management software.

About you:
- You are discreet, organised and a dependable colleague.

We offer a modern office, flexible hours and support for further legal study.
