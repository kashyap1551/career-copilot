Research Scientist in Environmental Data Science
Tarnwick Institute for Environmental Research

The Tarnwick Institute for Environmental Research studies how land use and climate change affect water and soil. We are looking for a research scientist to lead the data analysis of a new multi-year monitoring project.

Required:
- PhD in ecology, statistics, environmental science or a closely related field.
- Strong experience with statistical modelling, ideally Bayesian or mixed-effects models.
- Proficiency in R or Python for reproducible data analysis.
- Experience with spatial data and geographic information systems.
- A record of peer-reviewed publications in international journals.
- Experience writing grant proposals or contributing to funded projects.

A plus:
- Experience supervising master's or doctoral students.
- Experience with high-performance computing clusters.

About you:
- You are independent, collaborative and committed to open science.

We offer a permanent research position, funding for conferences and a collaborative, international team.
