Research profile
Quantitative ecologist working on how land use and climate change reshape freshwater and grassland communities. My work combines long-term field surveys, remote sensing and hierarchical statistical models, and I try to make every analysis reproducible from raw data to figure.

Education
PhD in Ecology, University of Tarnwick, 2015-2019. Thesis: Drivers of invertebrate community change in lowland streams. Supervisors: Prof. A. Lindqvist and Dr B. Oyelaran.
MSc Environmental Science, University of Northvale, 2012-2014. Distinction.
BSc Biology, University of Northvale, 2009-2012.

Senior Postdoctoral Researcher, Institute of Landscape Ecology, University of Tarnwick, 2022-present
- Lead the statistical work package of a five-country project on grassland biodiversity and manage a team of two doctoral students and one technician.
- Built a Bayesian hierarchical model of species richness across 1,200 survey plots and published the code and data in an open repository.
