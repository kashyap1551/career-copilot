Site Supervisor, Residential Building
Tarnwick Homes Construction, Musterstadt

Tarnwick Homes Construction builds mid-sized residential blocks in and around Musterstadt. We need an experienced site supervisor to run one of our building sites from groundworks to handover.

Required:
- At least five years of experience supervising residential or commercial construction sites.
- Able to coordinate subcontractors and keep to the construction schedule.
- Experience with quantity take-off (Aufmass) and checking subcontractor invoices.
- Knowledge of site safety rules and experience running safety briefings.
- Able to read structural and architectural drawings.

A plus:
- Experience with building information modelling.
- Experience with the German construction contract rules (VOB).

About you:
- You are a reliable team player with a hands-on approach.

We offer a company vehicle, a permanent contract and further training. Send your application to recruiter@example.org.
